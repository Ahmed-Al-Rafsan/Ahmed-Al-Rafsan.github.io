About

Three backgrounds, one thread.

📷Add your photo here

I didn't arrive at data through a straight computer-science line. I studied Aeronautical Engineering, then did an MBA focused on Human Resource Management, then went back for a Master's in Business Analytics. Each one taught me something the others didn't — how to think in systems, how to read people, and how to turn both into a decision a business will actually act on.

Today I work as a Data Analyst in Melbourne. Most of my week is spent doing the unglamorous part of the job well: cleaning messy data, asking the right question of it, and building the dashboard or model that turns "here's some numbers" into "here's what we should do."

I'm now building toward AI Security. As companies put more of their operations behind AI systems, the ones who understand both the data layer and the security layer will be the ones worth hiring. I'm working through a Security+ certification alongside my current projects, graduating this July, with sights set on contributing to a team at a company operating at serious scale.
